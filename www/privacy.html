taku · Privacy Policy

Effective July 23, 2026

The short version

taku collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Everything you do in taku — your swipes, ratings, lists, profile, and rank — is stored only on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your profile (display name, handle, avatar, bio)
  • Your lists: Want to Watch, Watching, and tier ratings
  • Your taste model (the genre preferences taku learns from your swipes)
  • Friend entries you add via share codes

None of this is transmitted to us or anyone else. We could not read it if we wanted to — taku has no server of its own.

Network requests taku makes

taku fetches anime catalog data (titles, artwork, air dates, characters) from the public AniList API. These requests contain no account information or personal identifiers — like any internet request, they necessarily include your IP address, which taku does not log and cannot access. AniList's own privacy policy governs their servers.

Sharing features

Rank-card images, friend codes, and data backups are generated on your device and shared only when you choose to share them, through your device's own share or save functions. taku never uploads them anywhere.

Children

taku does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children — because it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes

If taku's privacy practices ever change (for example, if optional accounts are added someday), this policy will be updated here and the app's store listing privacy label will be updated to match before any such feature ships.
